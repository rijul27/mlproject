Exploratory Data Analysis

What did we discover?

EDA was used to understand data quality, distributions, categorical relationships and correlations before model training.

Why EDA matters

Exploratory Data Analysis helps us understand the structure of the dataset before applying machine learning. It allows us to inspect distributions, compare groups, identify relationships between variables and check whether the data contains quality issues that need to be addressed during preprocessing.

01

Dataset Structure

The dataset contains 1,000 student records and 8 columns. It includes 5 categorical features, 2 numerical input features and the mathematics score as the target.

02

Data Quality

The dataset was inspected for missing values, duplicated rows and data types before moving into the machine learning pipeline.

03

Score Distribution

The average-score distribution is roughly bell-shaped, with most students having scores in the approximately 60–80 range.

04

Mathematics Score

Mathematics has a lower minimum score compared with reading and writing and contains more extremely low observations.

05

Reading vs Mathematics

Students with stronger reading scores generally tend to have stronger mathematics scores, indicating a strong positive relationship.

06

Writing vs Mathematics

Writing scores also show a strong positive relationship with mathematics performance.

07

Subject Correlation

Mathematics, reading and writing have strong positive correlations, approximately in the 0.8–0.85 range.

08

Test Preparation

Students who completed the test preparation course perform better across mathematics, reading and writing.

09

Lunch Analysis

Students receiving standard lunch have higher average scores compared with students receiving free/reduced lunch.

10

Parental Education

Students whose parents have bachelor's or master's degrees show higher average academic performance.

11

Race / Ethnicity

Group E shows the highest average performance, while Group A tends to show the lowest average performance across the subjects.

12

Gender Analysis

Female students lead in overall average performance, while male students show slightly higher average mathematics scores.

EDA Takeaways

What does this mean for the model?

These observations help explain why the selected features can be useful for predicting mathematics performance.

Academic Scores Are Important

The strong relationship between reading, writing and mathematics suggests that reading and writing scores contain useful predictive information for mathematics performance.

Categorical Features Add Context

Lunch type, parental education, test preparation and race/ethnicity show differences in average performance and therefore provide additional context to the prediction problem.

Preprocessing Is Necessary

Categorical variables must be converted into numerical representations and numerical variables need appropriate scaling before they are passed to the machine learning models.
